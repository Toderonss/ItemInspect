# ItemInspect CHANGELOG

## v1.0.0
- Initial release
- Inspect the item in your hands with a key (F by default), CS2-style
- Separate animations for different kinds of items: Classic, Flip, Toss, Gentle, Lift, Heavy, Blade
- Small items (berries, mushrooms, etc.) are inspected with one hand; the left hand lets go and comes back smoothly
- Hold the key to loop the inspection, press again to restart it
- Knife swing on LMB for blades without their own LMB action (e.g. Ritual Dagger)
- "Inspect" and "Swing" hints next to the game's item hints
- Supports letters, Alt / Ctrl / Shift and mouse buttons as the inspect key
